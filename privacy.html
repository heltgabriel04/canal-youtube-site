Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This application is a private tool used only by its owner to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.

Data we access

The app requests permission only to upload videos (scope youtube.upload) to the channel of the Google account that authorizes it. It does not read, collect or store personal data from any other user.

Data storage and sharing

Authorization tokens are stored as private secrets in the owner's own account. No data is sold, shared with or disclosed to third parties.

Google API Services

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
